What to try

  1. Choose any of the 10 client reports, then select one or several clients. Search, filters, groups, sorting and pagination work.
  2. Switch Download / Email now / Schedule. Try custom dates, a fixed snapshot date, financial year, folio, PMS opening date and Holdings sections.
  3. Create a schedule, then edit, pause/resume, run or delete it from Schedules. Inspect completed jobs in History.
  4. Use “Prototype controls” to simulate loading, no clients, generation failure, no data or partial success.
  5. Resize to a phone width. Test keyboard navigation, Escape to close a modal, and selection persistence.
